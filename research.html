Reaction mechanisms

How do templates guide macrocycle formation?

My crown ether studies examine pathways in the synthesis of dibenzo-18-crown-6 and benzo-21-crown-7, including intermediates, transition states, and metal-ion template effects.

Photochemistry & excited states

What governs a molecular photoswitch?

My work on azo crown ethers investigates photochemical and thermal isomerization, including strained cyclic systems and potassium-coordinated butterfly crown ethers. Related work examines N-oxide photochemistry and polyene photodynamics.
